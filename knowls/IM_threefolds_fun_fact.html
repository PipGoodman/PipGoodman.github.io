Let $A$ be an abelian threefold defined over a number field $K$ with endomorphism algebra an imaginary quadratic field $M$. Then there exists an elliptic curve $E/K$ with CM by $M$ such that the Tate twist $V_\ell(E)(1)$ is a subrepresentation of $\wedge^3V_\ell(A)$.

Appealing to theory of CM elliptic curves, we find $\#\mathrm{Cl}(M) \leq [K \colon \mathbb{Q}]$. In particular, for $K$ of fixed degree, there are only finitely many possibilities for $M$.
