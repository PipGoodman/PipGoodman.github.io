Let $\ell$ be a prime, $V$ be a 3-dimensional vector space over $\mathbb{F}_{\ell^2}$ and $\langle, \rangle \colon V \times V \rightarrow \mathbb{F}_{\ell^2}^*$ be a non-degenerate Hermitian pairing. Let $\Gamma \mathrm{U}_3(\ell)$ denote the group of $\mathbb{F}_{\ell^2}$-semilinear transformations of $V$ such that for any $g \in \Gamma \mathrm{U}_3(\ell)$ there exists $\lambda \in \mathbb{F}_{\ell^2}^*$ and $\sigma \in \mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{F}_{\ell^2}/\mathbb{F}_{\ell})$ satisfying \[ \langle gv,gw \rangle = \lambda \sigma(\langle v, w \rangle) \] for all $v,w \in V$. For $\ell \not \equiv 1,25,121 \pmod{168}$ the group $\Gamma \mathrm{U}_3(\ell)$ is a Galois group over $\mathbb{Q}$.
